About Surflist

Surflist is a curated directory of surf schools, shops, stays and services, organised by country, region and town.

Every listing is checked against the business's own official website.

If we can't confirm a business is real and operating from its own site, it isn't listed.

Founded in 2026 by Ben Manton.

Everything surfing, on the blog.

If you run a surf business, get listed.
