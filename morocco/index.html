Surfing in Morocco

One of the most accessible warm-water surf destinations from Europe — centred on Taghazout Bay near Agadir, with world-class right-hand point breaks, year-round sunshine and a dense cluster of surf camps, schools and shops, plus mellower beginner waves up the coast at Essaouira.
