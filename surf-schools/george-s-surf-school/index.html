George's Surf School

George's Surf School is a surf school in Polzeath, Cornwall, England.

Private-only surf coaching school at Polzeath, run by George Stoy — a professional surf and alpine instructor who lives between Polzeath and Champéry, Switzerland. Instructors are ISA / Surfing GB qualified coaches and international beach lifeguards, all over 25 and trained by George. Teaching is 1:1 or small private groups, in English, French, Spanish or German. Meeting point is Busty Moo's Magic Surf Hire at the Polzeath beach car park entrance; lessons also meet at Harlyn Bay (steps from the grass car park) and Constantine Bay (café in the car park). Clothing shop via Shopify; gift vouchers; Polzeath Ladies Surf Club.

Services

  • Private 1:1 and small-group surf coaching (beginner, improver, longboard, shortboard)
  • Children's ocean introduction and age-banded kids coaching
  • Video coaching and surf analysis
  • Immersive weekend / 3-day / 5-day courses (residential in spring and autumn; non-residential year-round)
  • ISA Level 1 and Level 2 surf coach qualifications
  • 2026 surf trips (Morocco, Costa Rica) and Wine & Surf retreat at The Point, Polzeath
  • Clothing and accessories shop
  • Gift vouchers
  • Polzeath Ladies Surf Club

Lessons

  • Private coaching: 2-hour lesson, 3-hour half day, or 6-hour day (max 6 people)
  • Children's Introduction to the Ocean: 1 hour, ages 5–6 (max 2 children; 1:1 recommended)
  • Age-banded children's lessons: 7–9 (2 hours, max 4:1); 9–11; 12–14; 14–16
  • Video coaching: 2 hours (max 4)
  • Beginner lesson in waist-depth water; goal is riding a wave for 5 seconds

Camps

  • Immersive courses: weekend/2-day beginner; 3- or 5-day improver and advanced (3 hours water + 1 hour theory per day; video on improver/advanced). Minimum 3, maximum 6 people, low season only
