Ollie's Surf Centre

Ollie's Surf Centre is a surf school in Lahinch, Clare, Ireland.

Ollie Welsh has run this Lahinch school since 2003 from a beachfront base by the Seaworld Leisure Centre, a few metres from the sand. Instructors are beach-lifeguard qualified and the centre keeps softboards and wetsuits on hand year-round for lessons and hire.

Services

  • Beginner and progressive surf lessons
  • Kids summer surf camps
  • Surfboard and wetsuit rentals
  • Team building, corporate, school, college, hen and stag packages
