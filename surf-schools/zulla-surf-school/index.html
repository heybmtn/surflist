Zulla Surf School

Zulla Surf School is a surf school in Nazaré, Silver Coast, Portugal.

Zulla Surf School sits inside Zulla Nazaré Surf Village, a surf-camp-style hotel created by local founders including Bodyboard World Champion Teresa Almeida. Guests can combine stays with lessons, then unwind at the terrace bar or bowls café a short hop from Nazaré’s waves.

Services

  • Group surf lessons
  • Private surf lessons
  • Surf accommodation packages
  • Surf village stay (hotel / surf camp style)

Lessons

  • Group surf lessons for children and adults (accredited instructors; equipment and sports insurance included)
  • Private surf lessons (one student; equipment and sports insurance included)

Camps

  • Pack Surfer — min 7 nights shared room with breakfast; 10 surf lessons; available April–October
  • Pack Adventures — min 7 nights shared room with breakfast; 5 surf lessons plus yoga, horse ride on Praia do Norte, dolphin visit and terrace/bowls drinks; available April–October
  • Pack Big Waves — winter package with shared room, breakfast, 2 surf lessons and guided boat visit to the Nazaré canyon (Nov–Mar; trip not included)
