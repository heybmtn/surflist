Tramore Surf School

Tramore Surf School is a surf school in Tramore, Waterford, Ireland.

Surf shop and school on Tramore promenade (est. 2009), opposite the lifeguard hut on the 5 km Blue Flag beach — ISA-approved lessons, kids' camps, hire and gear sales.
