Algarve Surf School

Algarve Surf School is a surf school in Sagres, Algarve, Portugal.

Algarve Surf School has taught in Sagres since 1996, pairing ASI-certified coaching with a long-running surf hostel and beach hut at Praia do Amado. Lessons run year-round for beginners through intermediates and families, with daily beach choice driven by wind and swell around Sagres and Carrapateira.

Services

  • Group surf lessons
  • Private surf lessons
  • Kids surf lessons
  • Surf & Stay camp weeks
  • Bed & breakfast hostel stay
  • Surfboard and wetsuit hire
  • Surf shop in Sagres
  • Beach transport (add-on)

Lessons

  • Surf lessons — 1-, 3- or 5-day packages; 2-hour lessons per day; board, wetsuit and sports insurance included; levels from first-wave beginner through green-wave progression
  • Private surf lessons — individuals or small groups up to 5 people
  • Kids surf lessons — ages 6–12; small groups up to 5; morning sessions focused on fun and ocean awareness
  • Family-friendly lessons for kids 6+ and parents

Rentals

  • Surfboard and wetsuit hire (Sagres shop and Amado beach hut)

Camps

  • Surf Camp Week — 7 nights + 5-day surf course (2 lessons per day), breakfasts, beach lunch packs, equipment, beach transport, sports insurance; yoga Apr–Nov; Lagos-to-camp transfer
  • New Year’s Eve Surf Week — same structure as camp week with NYE celebration
  • Bed & Breakfast stays (minimum 2 nights; shared or private rooms)

When to go

Surf camp weeks and lessons run year-round. Smaller friendly surf in spring/summer; more powerful swells in autumn/winter. Yoga sessions with camp packages April–November.
