    Gower Surf Academy

    Gower Surf Academy is a surf school in Caswell Bay, Gower, Wales.

    South Welsh surf company at Caswell Beach. On the Approved Outdoor activity providers list for the City and County of Swansea; coaches described as qualified lifeguards and ISA surf coaches with enhanced CRB checks. Purchased and renamed from the long-running Gower Surfing / Gower Surfing Development school.

    Services

    • Group, family, student and 1:1 surfing sessions
    • Kids Sunday squads and weekday after-school sessions
    • Summer Holiday Kidz Club
    • Birthday parties and stag/hen parties
    • Gift vouchers
