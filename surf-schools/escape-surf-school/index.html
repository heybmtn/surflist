Escape Surf School

Escape Surf School is a surf school in Newquay, Cornwall, England.

Escape is a specialist surf-only school — no kayak, coasteering or SUP lessons — on the seaview terrace of Belushi's / St Christopher's at 35 Fore Street, overlooking Towan.

Head coach Mike Young is an ex-pro (BSA L4 / ISA L3); senior coach Reubin Pearce is also an ex-pro. Coaches are ISA L1–2 and RLSS. Purpose-built facilities opened in August 2018.

Open 12 months a year, seven days a week, except Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day. Lessons are 2 hours, with kit included.
