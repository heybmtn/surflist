Surf Riders & Co

Surf Riders & Co is a surf school in Ericeira, Lisbon, Portugal.

Surf Riders & Co Ericeira has taught surfing and Portuguese surf lifestyle since 2016, with a year-round shop and lesson meeting point on Rua do Carmo and private surf-camp villas nearby. The team offers beginner to advanced coaching, SUP, yoga and stay packages in Europe's World Surfing Reserve.

  • Kids surf lessons

  • Surf shop
  • Transfers
  • Retreats and group bookings
