Surfaventura

Surfaventura is a surf school in Matosinhos, Porto, Portugal.

Surfaventura is a Matosinhos beachfront school open since 2003, with facilities under the promenade about 200 metres north of the Anémona roundabout and direct beach access. Beyond year-round lessons it runs holiday camps, team building, birthday parties and group programmes for schools, companies and Erasmus students, and keeps a small shop for new and used kit.

Services

  • Surf, bodyboard, SUP and skate lessons (group and private)
  • Equipment rental (boards, wetsuits, SUP, skate)
  • Holiday camps (Campos de Férias)
  • Team building, birthday parties and stag/hen programmes
  • Surf for schools, Erasmus and private groups
  • After-work surf sessions (summer months)
  • Surf trips to neighbouring beaches
  • New and used surf shop
  • Experience vouchers

Hours
Open year-round, weekdays and weekends; typically from about 09:30 until near sunset (around 18:00 in winter, 20:00 in summer), including lunchtime. Closed on major holidays including Christmas, Easter, New Year’s Day and São João (24 June).

Group lessons typically 4–6+ people; private lessons for 1–3 (surf/bodyboard/SUP) or 1–2 (skate).
Equipment
Lesson price includes experienced instructor, board/wetsuit (and fins/skate gear as relevant), facility access, required licences and insurance (IVA included). Prices in force from 1 June 2024; lesson packs valid 6 months.
