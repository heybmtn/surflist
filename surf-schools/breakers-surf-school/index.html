Breakers Surf School

Breakers Surf School is a surf school in St Agnes, Cornwall, England.

Owner-run school at Trevaunance Cove, St Agnes, led by Tom Roberson (ISA Level 2, HND Surf Science). Instructors are ISA-qualified, beach lifeguards, enhanced-DBS and safeguarding trained. Operating base is Quay Road by Reppers Coombe car park; postal address is 1 Kew Breanek. Also offers SUP around Trevaunance and Trevellas, a term-time Local Kids Surf Academy, school-group programmes, and yoga packages with Georgina Fearn.
