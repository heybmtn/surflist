Buen Surf School

Buen Surf School is a surf school in Gran Canaria, Canary Islands, Spain.

Buen Surf School has operated since 2016 beside La Cicer beach in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, founded by Javier Antúnez after leaving a corporate career for a dedicated surf lifestyle school. The beachfront base sits on the city’s north shore next to one of Europe’s best-known urban surf beaches, with a focus on respect, dedication and consistency in the water.
