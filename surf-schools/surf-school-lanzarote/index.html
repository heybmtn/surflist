Surf School Lanzarote

Surf School Lanzarote is a surf school in Lanzarote, Canary Islands, Spain.

Long-running Famara school in the Surf Insight group, known for ISA-qualified coaching from complete beginners through intermediate, advanced and longboard. Courses emphasise more in-water time and a structured coaching system praised by high-level coaches.
