Nazaré Surf School

Nazaré Surf School is a surf school in Nazaré, Silver Coast, Portugal.

Founded in 2012, Nazaré Surf School is a beachfront school on the main Nazaré beach run by local certified instructors, some from competition and lifeguard backgrounds. It is set up as a physical beach base with changing rooms, showers, lockers, and wheelchair access, teaching in English, French, Spanish, and Portuguese.

Services

  • Surf and bodyboard lessons (first-timers through advanced)
  • Equipment rental (surfboards, bodyboards, SUP, wetsuits)
  • Gift vouchers (private and group lessons)
  • Lesson photography and video
  • Special events and team building

Lessons

  • Group surf lessons — 90 minutes; include surfboard, wetsuit and insurance; warm-up and beach induction with level-based teacher assignment
  • Private lessons — 1 or 2 people; closer attention for first-timers or skill improvement

Rentals

  • Surfboard rental (soft tops and hard tops)
  • Surf kit (board and suit)
  • Bodyboard kit (board, suit and fins)
  • Stand-up paddle boards
  • Wetsuits
