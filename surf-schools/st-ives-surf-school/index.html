    St Ives Surf School

    St Ives Surf School is a surf school in St Ives, Cornwall, England.

    St Ives’ surf HQ sits on Blue Flag Porthmeor, a short walk from town and the Tate, recognised by Surfing England as a Centre of Excellence. A second watersports centre on Porthminster runs paddleboard, kayak and (when needed) coasteering. Partners with Surfers Against Sewage and The Wave Project; co-founded Saints Boardriders (150+ members) and a surf house overlooking Porthmeor. Live beach webcam on site.

    Services

    • Surf lessons levels 1–5 (beginner, improver, advanced, private)
    • Coasteering
    • Paddleboard lessons and tours
    • Sea kayak tours
    • Surf hire at Porthmeor; SUP/kayak hire at Porthminster
    • Clothing and accessories shop
    • Gift vouchers
    • Salt water / luxury surf retreats (enquiry form)
    • Saints Boardriders club and surf house
    • Educational group sessions

    Lessons

    • Beginner surf: 2 hours, waist-deep water
    • Improver surf: 2 hours
    • Advanced: typically private or matched-ability group
    • Private surf: 1.5 hours, 1:1 up to large groups
    • Paddleboard beginner 1.5 hours; kayak tour 1.5–2 hours; coasteering 1.5–2 hours
    • Tide-dependent start times; Porthmeor does not break at high tide
    • Arrive 30 minutes early to check in

    Rentals

    • Porthmeor: wetsuits (5mm), softboards 7–9ft, hardboards (50+ boards, 5ft fish to 9ft longboard; Torq, Softech, Ocean Earth, Alder), bodyboards, boots/gloves/hoods, deckchairs and windbreaks
    • Porthminster (May–October, weather dependent): Red Paddle Co inflatable SUPs, single and double sit-on-top kayaks (buoyancy aids included), bodyboards, wetsuits
    • ID required; parental consent under 16; long-term hire (3+ days) can be reserved

    Camps

    • Salt water luxury surf retreats (enquiry only — no public tariff on pages checked)

    Porthmeor BeachPorthminster Beach

    When to go

    Porthmeor year-round. Porthminster watersports April–October. RNLI: Porthmeor Easter–October; Porthminster May–September.

    Details

    Location
    Porthmeor Beach, St Ives, TR26 1JZ, St Ives, Cornwall
    Country
    England
    Hours
    Both centres: 09:30–17:30, 7 days. Porthmeor HQ open all year. Porthminster watersports April–October (closed in poor weather or strong winds).
    Group size
    Maximum group size 1–7.
    Min age
    Children under 10 need a non-surfing adult in group lessons (free wetsuit). Hire/other copy: children under 8 accompanied by a non-surfing adult. Coasteering minimum age 12. Contact St Ives Surf School to confirm surf min age.
    Equipment
    Lessons include winter wetsuit, surfboard, boots, gloves and hood in winter, suncream, changing rooms and shower. Water time around 80 minutes in a 2-hour lesson. Hire: 5/3mm wetsuits (Alder, SRFACE, C-Skins).
