Silver Coast Surf School

Silver Coast Surf School is a surf school in Baleal, Peniche, Portugal.

Licensed Baleal surf school founded in 2010, run by FPS-certified head coach Gonçalo Caldeira with a team focused on level-split groups and carefully chosen spots. Combines year-round lessons and rentals with Surfhouse Helena camp accommodation a short walk from local beaches.

Services

  • Group surf lessons (Mon–Fri)
  • Private surf lessons (any day)
  • Surf equipment rentals
  • Guided surf trips for advanced surfers
  • Lisbon airport transfer
  • Accommodation (Surfhouse Helena / Silver Coast beach residence and partners)

Lessons

  • Group lessons Mon–Fri for students over 14: Apr–Jun/Oct and Jul–Sep packages include 2 x 2-hour sessions per day (theory + practice), board, wetsuit, beach transfer and insurance; Nov–Mar 1 session/day
  • Private lessons any day from age 8: 2-hour session with board, wetsuit and transfer
  • Recommended week: 5 days Mon–Fri with video analysis at camp on longer programmes
  • Weekend private sessions available if booking extra surf

Rentals

  • Surfboard + wetsuit
  • Skateboard

Camps

  • Silver Coast Beach Residence (partner boutique residence)
