Ori Surf

Ori Surf is a surf school in Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain.

Local El Médano school beside Playa de la Jaquita with a strong Google review score and coaches who grew up on Tenerife’s south swell. Alongside standard coaching they run free adapted-surf sessions for people with disabilities.

Services

  • Surf lessons
  • Board and wetsuit rental
  • Wingfoil lessons
  • Adapted surfing (free for people with disabilities)

Lessons

  • 2-hour group surf courses
  • Private surf
  • Pack of 2 classes
  • Pack of 3 classes
  • Full-week beginner pack (3 courses + photos + gear for 7 days)
  • Wingfoil lessons (1–3 days, small groups)

Rentals

  • Surfboard rental (2 hours, half day or full day)
  • Wetsuit rental

Prices
