Blackhouse Watersports

Blackhouse Watersports is a surf school in Tiree, Argyll, Scotland.

Blackhouse Watersports is a family-run surf and kitesurf school based in a beach hut on Balevullin Beach on the Isle of Tiree. The island’s multi-aspect white-sand beaches and Gulf Stream–warmed water make it a low-key Inner Hebridean base for beginners through advanced riders when Atlantic swell and wind align.

Services

  • Surf school (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
  • Kitesurf lessons and packages
  • Equipment hire

Lessons

  • Beginner, intermediate and advanced surf lessons — twice daily depending on tides; all equipment included
  • Beginner, intermediate and advanced/wave kitesurf lessons — small groups of 2–4; ages 12+ (under 18s with parent/guardian)

Rentals

  • Surf equipment hire (soft tops, performance shortboards, mid-lengths and longboards)

Prices

  • Surf lesson £40 per lesson (equipment included)
  • Kitesurf lesson £150 per hour (up to 4 people)
