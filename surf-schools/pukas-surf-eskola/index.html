Pukas Surf Eskola

Pukas Surf Eskola is a surf school in San Sebastián, Basque Country, Spain.

The school arm of the iconic Basque surf brand Pukas, teaching on Zurriola since 1982 with top facilities, the widest rental quiver in town and lessons for all levels.
