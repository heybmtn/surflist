Pukas Peña Txuri Surf Eskola

Pukas Peña Txuri Surf Eskola is a surf school in Sopelana, Basque Country, Spain.

Beachfront Pukas school on Sopelana’s Blue Flag Arrietara beach, teaching from first waves through competition coaching. Summer camps for ages 4–16 mix surfing with outdoor sports, and adapted surfing sessions welcome participants of all abilities.

  • Adapted surfing
  • Advanced / improvement coaching
  • Competition and high-performance training
  • Summer camps and training camps
