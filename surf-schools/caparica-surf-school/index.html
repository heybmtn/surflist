    Caparica Surf School

    Caparica Surf School is a surf school in Costa da Caparica, Lisbon, Portugal.

    Caparica Surf School is based at Apoio de Praia N°24 on Nova Praia in Costa da Caparica and presents itself as a federação-certified school with about 25 years of experience. Lessons run about 90 minutes with theory, warm-up and water time; insurance and kit are included. The school also runs national and international surf trips plus birthday parties, team building and free-time (ATL) activities.
