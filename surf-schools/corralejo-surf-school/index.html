Corralejo Surf School

Corralejo Surf School is a surf school in Fuerteventura, Canary Islands, Spain.

Federation-certified surf school based in Corralejo on Fuerteventura’s north coast, teaching beginners through intermediate and private clients. The school pairs lessons with optional stays at its nearby surfhouse villa overlooking the dunes.
