Ocean Life Surf School

Ocean Life Surf School is a surf school in Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain.

Canarian Surf Federation-certified school at Playa de las Américas in south Tenerife, with small groups (max four per instructor), a surf shop and sunrise and sunset sessions.
