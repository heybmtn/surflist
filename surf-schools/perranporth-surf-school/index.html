Perranporth Surf School

Perranporth Surf School is a surf school in Perranporth, Cornwall, England.

Longest-established surf school in Perranporth, family-run by Gav and Kate since 2004. Surfing England affiliated; coaches are Surfing England / ISA qualified and beach lifeguards. Summer pitch is on the sand; from September–May the published winter base is Piran Surf, 38 St. Piran’s Road.

Services

  • Open-group beginner and improver lessons (single and multi-lesson packages)
  • Family packages (one participant aged 7–16 required for family rates)
  • Group discounts from three surfers
  • Private tuition (1–8 surfers)
  • School / college / youth / guide / scout groups
  • Birthday, corporate, stag and hen sessions

Lessons

  • Open-group beginner & improver, 2 hours practical (plus check-in 20 minutes early; plan three hours arrival to departure)
  • Family open-group packages (2–5 people advertised; add surfers)
  • Group packages (3–10, 11–20, 21+)
  • Stag/hen and birthday party sessions
  • Private 1–8 surfers (aimed at advanced beginners / intermediates; beginners steered to open-group)
  • School trips and activity weeks (enquire)

Surf spots served

Perranporth Beach

When to go

On the beach May–September. Winter location September–May at Piran Surf. Two daily 2-hour lessons in summer, primary times 10am and 2pm, adjusted for tide.
