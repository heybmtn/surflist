Onda Pura

Onda Pura is a surf school in Matosinhos, Porto, Portugal.

Onda Pura is one of Portugal’s longest-running surf centres, based on Praia de Matosinhos since 1994. Beyond everyday coaching it runs a competition-focused training pathway, kids’ holiday camps, corporate days and Porto-area surf camps with airport transfers.

Services

  • Surf, SUP, surf skate and wing foil lessons
  • Equipment rentals
  • Kids’ summer holiday camps
  • Birthday parties
  • Surf camps with accommodation
  • Corporate events
  • Surf Bus (pickup + group lesson)
  • Competition training project (Projeto Formação)

Lessons

  • Group and private surf lessons (initiation through competition)
  • Surf lesson packs (4, 8 or 12 classes)
  • Stand-up paddle group, private and river/sea tours
  • Surf skate classes, packs and tours
  • Wing foil private, semi-private and guiding

Rentals

  • Surf soft/hard boards and 4/3 wetsuits (from age 6)
  • SUP boards and wetsuits
  • Surf skate / longboard with protection
  • Wing foil kit (board, wing, foil, impact vest, helmet)
  • Beach tennis nets, rackets and balls

Camps

  • Summer holiday camps (ages 8–18): full-time or part-time beach multi-activity weeks
  • Surf Camp packages with hostel, guesthouse or hotel stays (Sun–Sat)
  • Includes two surf lessons per day, gear access, airport transfer and insurance
