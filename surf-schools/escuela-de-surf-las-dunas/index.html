Escuela de Surf Las Dunas

Escuela de Surf Las Dunas is a surf school in Salinas, Asturias, Spain.

Escuela de Surf Las Dunas (Family Surfers) runs year-round lessons and surfcamps on Salinas beach, with a club surf house and reduced-group coaching for adults and children. The operation also covers skatecamp days and equipment hire, and customer service hours are published as 10:00–14:00 and 16:00–20:00.

Services

  • Surf lessons (all levels, adults and children)
  • Surfcamps (adults and minors, year-round packages)
  • Surf house / club accommodation
  • Equipment rental (surf and SUP)
  • Skatecamp
  • Private surf club membership

Lessons

  • Single group surf class — 2 hours; board, wetsuit and insurance included
  • Multi-day group courses — 3, 4, 5, 7 or 10 days (2 hours per class); board, wetsuit and insurance included
  • Perfectionamiento weekend (Sat–Sun) or 5-day improver blocks — 2 hours of surf per day
  • Winter surf school — monthly class packs (4, 8 or 12 classes)

Rentals

  • Half day: surfboard + leash €15; wetsuit €5
  • Full day: surfboard + leash €20; wetsuit €5
  • Half day SUP €30 + wetsuit €5 (€35 total)
  • Full day SUP €45 + wetsuit €5 (€50 total)

Camps

  • Year-round surfcamps for adults and minors on Salinas beach (holiday, bridge and summer packages with full board or B&B options)
  • Kids summer surfcamps (July packages listed)
  • Special bridge/holiday camps (Semana Santa, May bridges, Halloween/November, December)
