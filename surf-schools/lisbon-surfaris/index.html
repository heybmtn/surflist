Lisbon Surfaris

Lisbon Surfaris is a surf school in Carcavelos, Lisbon, Portugal.

Lisbon Surfaris runs lessons, rentals and surf-and-stay packages from a shop inside Carcavelos Beach train station, about 15 minutes from Lisbon, with the beach school beside Casa da Praia. Local certified instructors also offer kids’ clinics, a Lisbon boat-and-surf outing, and Cascais or Ericeira stay packages.

Services

  • Group surf lessons
  • Private surf lessons
  • Lesson packs
  • Surf clinics for kids
  • Surf & boat tour from Lisbon
  • Surfboard, wetsuit and bike rentals
  • Cascais and Ericeira surf & stay packages
  • Gift cards

Lessons

  • Group surf lesson — about 2 hours; max 5 students per instructor; board, wetsuit, leash and fins included
  • Private surf lesson — about 2 hours; 1-to-1
  • Group lesson pack of 5 (buy 4 get 1 free)
  • Private lesson pack of 5 (buy 4 get 1 free)
  • Shuttle from shop to beach and back; pick-ups also available

Rentals

  • Surfboards and wetsuits
  • Bike with surfboard rack
  • Bike rental
  • Leashes and fins with lesson/rental gear

Camps

  • Cascais Surf & Stay — accommodation with surf programme
  • Ericeira Surf & Stay — accommodation with surf programme
  • Surf Clinics for kids during most school breaks

Prices

  • Bike + board rental €30
  • Bike rental €20
  • Surf & Boat tour (boat tour + surf lesson, min 4 people) €120 per 4 hours
