Scarborough Surf School

Scarborough Surf School is a surf school in Scarborough, Yorkshire, England.

Long-running East Coast school at Cayton Bay, established in 1985 and now run with Secret Spot Surf Shop under owner Paul “Tomo” and head coach Tori. The beachfront base pairs ISA/SLSGB coaching with coasteering and SUP when the swell drops, plus kids’ clubs and hire year-round.

Services

  • Group and private surf lessons
  • Intermediate and advanced coaching
  • Kids clubs and Super Groms progression
  • Intensive / multi-week improver courses
  • Stand-up paddleboarding lessons and Super SUP
  • Coasteering
  • Surf equipment hire
  • Schools, scouts, university, corporate and military groups
  • Stag and hen parties
  • Become a surf coach courses
  • Gift vouchers
