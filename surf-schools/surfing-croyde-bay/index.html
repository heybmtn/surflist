Surfing Croyde Bay

Surfing Croyde Bay is a surf school in Croyde, Devon, England.

Purpose-built surf centre at Baggy Lodge on Moor Lane, led by Damon Beveridge, former GB / England junior head coach and director of Surf Development UK. A Surfing England Centre of Excellence with ISA-qualified coaches who are also beach lifeguards (two ISA Level 2 coaches on staff). Warm indoor changing, hot showers, lockers, sea-view Café Croyde Bay, licensed bar, on-site accommodation, and a Nordic wood-fired sauna with cold plunge overlooking the bay. Also used for elite / Team GB pathway coaching via Surf Development UK.

Services

  • Surf lessons (beginner, improver/advanced-beginner, intermediate, advanced)
  • Private 1:1 and private family/group lessons
  • Surf hire (boards, wetsuits, bodyboards, SUPs) with optional delivery
  • Coasteering
  • Weekend and one-day surf camps (adults and juniors)
  • Surf instructor / beach lifeguard training
  • Nordic sauna and ice bath
  • Café Croyde Bay
  • Gift cards
  • Stag/hen, schools, military, corporate / team-building

Lessons

  • 2.5-hour group surf lesson (beginner or improver)
  • 3-lesson Progressor (3 × 2.5 hours)
  • Intermediate group (2.5 hours, up to 6 people)
  • Private 1:1 (2.5 hours)
  • Private family/group (2.5 hours, up to 6 people)
  • Minimum 1.5 hours water time within the 2.5-hour lesson

Rentals

  • Surfboard hire (Softech softboards and Tiki improver boards; fleet 5'0"–9'0", 250+ boards)
  • Wetsuit hire (Tiki and SRFACE; 500+ suits, men’s/women’s/junior)
  • Bodyboard hire
  • Stand-up paddleboard hire
  • Door delivery available
  • 10% off hire for returning lesson customers / surf-school students

Camps

  • Weekend adult surf camp: 4 × 2.5-hour lessons over 2 days, lunch both days from Café Croyde Bay, group format, beginner–advanced
  • One-day adult surf camp (Saturday-only option of the two-day camp)
  • School-holiday junior camps: Groms 8–12 and Juniors 13–17, Easter, Whitsun and summer

Prices

  • Private 1:1 surf lesson £140 per lesson
