Habia Surfschool

Habia Surfschool is a surf school in Saint-Jean-de-Luz, French Basque Country, France.

Independent Basque-coast school led year-round by state-certified local coach Julien, who picks the day's best spot between Saint-Jean-de-Luz, Guéthary, Bidart and Hendaye. Small groups and personalised coaching keep sessions adapted to conditions and level.

  • Group surf lessons and stages
  • Private lessons
  • Longboard lessons
  • Year-round Habia Surf Club sessions
  • Surf guide half-day and full-day
  • EVJF / EVG and group events
