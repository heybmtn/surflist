Ocean Vertical

Ocean Vertical is a surf school in Belhaven Bay, East Lothian, Scotland.

Ocean Vertical is a B Corp–certified adventure company based at Hedderwick Hill Stables near Dunbar, running ethical outdoor trips across East Lothian and further into the Scottish Highlands. Surf sessions use Belhaven Bay and nearby east-coast beaches when the swell and weather line up, with coaches opening dates via a weekly wave mailing list rather than fixed slots.

Services

  • Surf lessons
  • Paddle boarding (SUP)
  • Coasteering
  • Yoga and surf / yoga and SUP day retreats
  • Climbing and mountaineering
  • Corporate and group adventures
  • Adventure camps
  • Nepal trekking expeditions

Lessons

  • Surf lessons — about 3 hours total with ~2 hours on water; beginner/intermediate; max 6 per group (min 3); age 12+; board, leash, wetsuit, boots, rash vest and transport to Belhaven Bay included
  • Private 1:1 surf sessions available on request for improvers exploring local spots
  • SUP Level 1 learn-to-paddle-board and Level 2 progression lessons

Camps

  • East Lothian Yoga and Surf Day Retreat
  • East Lothian Yoga and SUP Day Retreat
  • Mindfulness and paddle boarding day retreat
  • School-holiday Adventure Camps (with Foxlake Adventures)

Prices

  • Surf lesson £65 per person
  • East Lothian Yoga and Surf Day Retreat £135 per person
