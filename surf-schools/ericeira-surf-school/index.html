Ericeira Surf School

Ericeira Surf School is a surf school in Ericeira, Lisbon, Portugal.

Ericeira Surf School is run by Despomar, Lda and co-founded by local coaches Miguel Fortes and Michel Amaro, both identified on the site as Portuguese Surfing Federation instructors. The school presents itself as a way to learn, improve and have a real surfing experience in Ericeira, with optional photo and video from sessions and local guiding for intermediate and advanced surfers.

Services

  • Group lessons
  • Private lessons
  • Kids lessons
  • Surf tours
  • Ericeira Training Camps
  • Optional photo or video

Lessons

  • Group lessons — 90 min; beginners, intermediate and advanced; max 6 people per certified instructor; includes surfboard, wetsuit, insurance
  • Private lessons — 90 min; 1 person; beginners, intermediate and advanced; includes surfboard, wetsuit, insurance
  • Kids lessons — 90 min; beginners and intermediate; max 6 per certified instructor; includes surfboard, wetsuit, insurance
  • Surf tours — 5 hours; all levels; includes transport, surfboard, wetsuit, insurance, water, energetic bar

Camps

  • Ericeira Training Camps — 5 days, 9 surf sessions plus skating and physical training; video analysis; beginners, intermediate and advanced. Contact Ericeira Surf School to confirm.
