Progress Surf School

Progress Surf School is a surf school in Llangennith, Gower, Wales.

Surf school operating in Gower and Swansea since 2005. Homepage positions the team as a Surfing Wales Centre of Excellence, with lessons at Caswell Bay and Llangennith Beach.

Services

  • Surf lessons at Llangennith Beach and Caswell Bay
  • Progress Women sessions
  • SUP lessons
  • Kids after-school and school-holiday surf club
  • Kids surf parties
  • Gift vouchers
  • Shop

Lessons

  • Standard ~2-hour group (ages 8+; all equipment)
  • Full day (two 2-hour sessions)
  • 1:1 private
  • Large group / school / corporate / stag and hen
  • SUP lessons (age 10+)
  • Kids after-school club (Caswell; age 7+)
  • School holiday kids club (Caswell; ages 7–16)
