NQY Surf School

NQY Surf School is a surf school in Newquay, Cornwall, England.

NQY is a beach-front school on Great Western — the only school on that beach, there since 2014. It is led by Adam Griffiths, a four-time UK champion and World Tour longboarder, with centre manager Dale.

The on-sand centre has toilets, changing and lockers, plus a beach bar. RNLI cover the beach from May to September.

Hire is through sister site surfhirenewquay.co.uk. School groups go through First Wave Adventures. The school also offers coasteering at The Gazzle.
