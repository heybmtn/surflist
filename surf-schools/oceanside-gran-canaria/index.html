Oceanside Gran Canaria

Oceanside Gran Canaria is a surf school in Gran Canaria, Canary Islands, Spain.

Quiksilver & Roxy surf school on Las Canteras in Las Palmas, with SICTED quality recognition and twin bases on Numancia and Almansa streets. Beyond daily coaching they run Stay N’Surf packages, kids programmes and year-round hire on La Cícer.
