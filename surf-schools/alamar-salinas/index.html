Alamar Salinas

Alamar Salinas is a surf school in Salinas, Asturias, Spain.

Alamar Salinas combines a restored 1880s casa de indianos B&B on Calle Príncipe de Asturias with a beachside surf school a short walk from Salinas. Federation-certified coaches run group and private lessons for every age and level, plus adult and kids surf camps from the same house.

Services

  • Surf lessons (group and private)
  • Surf camps for adults and children
  • Surfboard and wetsuit rental
  • Boutique B&B / surf house accommodation
  • On-site restaurant

Lessons

  • Group surf class — 2 hours (about 20 minutes beach warm-up/theory, rest in water); board, wetsuit, lycra, changing and shower; RC and accident insurance included
  • Multi-day group packs — 2, 3 or 5 days (2 hours per day) and 10-class bono
  • Private lesson — 1 hour; board, wetsuit, changing, shower and insurance included
  • High season: lessons every day; off-season dates by phone or email

Rentals

  • Surfboard and wetsuit hire by half day or full day (prices on request / special rates for guests)

Camps

  • Surf Camp adultos
  • Surf Camp menores
  • Seasonal stay+surf packages (accommodation, daily breakfast, daily surf classes, gear for free surf after lessons)

Prices

  • Surf course 3 days (2 hr/day) €80
  • Surf course 5 days (2 hr/day) €120
