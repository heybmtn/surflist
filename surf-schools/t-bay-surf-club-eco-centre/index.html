T-Bay Surf Club & Eco Centre

T-Bay Surf Club & Eco Centre is a surf school in Tramore, Waterford, Ireland.

Ireland's oldest and largest surf club (founded 1967), the only purpose-built on-beach surf centre in Tramore — ISA- and European-Surfing-Federation-approved lessons for all ages.
