T-Bay Surf Club & Eco Centre

T-Bay Surf Club & Eco Centre is a surf school in Tramore, Waterford, Ireland.

Ireland’s longest-established surf club (founded 1967), based in a purpose-built beachfront centre at Tramore that opened in 1998 with changing rooms, training and meeting rooms, board storage and an observation area. Membership covers junior and senior surfing, Silver Surfers coaching and Irish Surfing affiliation rather than walk-up commercial lessons.

Services

  • Club membership (junior and senior)
  • Junior surf training
  • Masters / Silver Surfers coaching
  • Access to club boards and changing facilities
  • Club events and contests
  • On-site café
  • Club merchandise

Lessons

  • Junior surf training with voluntary qualified coaches
  • Masters classes for older new members
  • Silver Surfers coaching evenings (Thursdays ~6pm, ~1 hour; application required)
