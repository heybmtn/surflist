Lahinch Surf Experience

Lahinch Surf Experience is a surf school in Lahinch, Clare, Ireland.

Locally owned ISA-affiliated school on The Promenade in Lahinch, minutes from the Cliffs of Moher on the Wild Atlantic Way. Established in 2009, it runs adult, teen, kids, family and group sessions with Level 1 and Level 2 ISA coaches and beachfront kit hire.

Services

  • Adult, student, teen and kids surf lessons
  • Family and group surf lessons
  • Surfboard and wetsuit hire
  • Kids 5-day surf camps
  • Gift cards
