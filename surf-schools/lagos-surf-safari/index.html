Lagos Surf Safari

Lagos Surf Safari is a surf school in Lagos, Algarve, Portugal.

Lagos Surf Safari has run van-based surf trips from Lagos and Praia da Luz for over 17 years, choosing West or South Coast beaches by the day's conditions. ASI- and Portuguese Surfing Federation–accredited instructors keep sessions small, with transport, kit, snacks, and sports insurance built into each full day.

Services

  • Group surf lessons
  • Private surf lessons
  • Family surfing
  • Surf guiding / surf safari trips
  • Board and wetsuit rental with beach transfer (Rent and Ride)
  • Surf camp / retreats (on request)
  • Hostel accommodation (Banana Beach House)

Lessons

  • Group surf trips — full day; pickups typically 8:00–9:00 from Lagos area (Burgau / Praia da Luz on request); 2 × 1.5-hour sessions; max 8 students per instructor; beginners to advanced
  • Private lessons — max 2 students per instructor; includes transport, board, wetsuit, 2 × 90-minute sessions, sports insurance and snacks
  • Family surfing — children under 13 accompanied by an adult
  • Surf guiding days for experienced / pro surfers

Rentals

  • Rent and Ride — board, wetsuit, pickup and drop-off
  • Just a Ride — transfer only

Camps

  • Surf camps and retreats in Lagos (contact to arrange)
  • Surf weeks with accommodation at Banana Beach House hostel
