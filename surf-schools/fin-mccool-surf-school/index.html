Fin McCool Surf School

Fin McCool Surf School is a surf school in Rossnowlagh, Donegal, Ireland.

ISA-approved school, shop and beach-side stay set metres from Rossnowlagh’s Blue Flag sand, founded in 2006 by Neil Britton of the family that helped spark Irish surfing here in the 1960s. Three generations still share the lineup on good days, with changing rooms and a fully stocked shop next to the beach path.
