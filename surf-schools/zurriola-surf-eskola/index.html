Zurriola Surf Eskola

Zurriola Surf Eskola is a surf school in San Sebastián, Basque Country, Spain.

Opened in 2011 on Usandizaga street in Gros, Zurriola Surf Eskola is a year-round school a short walk from Zurriola beach. Federated instructors cover beginners through high performance for children, teens and adults, with academy terms, weekly blocks and custom sessions.

Services

  • Surf lessons
  • Weekly courses
  • Customised lessons
  • Surf academy (Sept–June)
  • Multisurf summer programme
  • School and group programmes
  • Equipment rental
  • Board storage
  • Physical conditioning

Lessons

  • Group lessons by age and level
  • Private and small-group customised lessons
  • Weekly Monday–Friday courses
  • Academy lessons (1–3 per week, 1h30)
  • Multisurf for children (summer)

Rentals
