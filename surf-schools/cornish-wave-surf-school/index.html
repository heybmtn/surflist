    Cornish Wave Surf School

    Cornish Wave Surf School is a surf school in Newquay, Cornwall, England.

    Cornish Wave is a Newquay surf and outdoor-activity school, operating since 2012. Lessons run at Towan Beach or Fistral Beach depending on tide and swell — the school says it is one of only two licensed to teach on Towan.

    The base is 40 Fore Street, rebuilt in 2026 as a purpose-built surf school and activity centre with indoor changing rooms, heated showers, outdoor changing and storage. Instructors are ISA / Surfing England Level 2+ and RLSS beach lifeguards.

    Group surf lessons are 2 hours, with a maximum of 8 people, for ages 8 and up (family and private options from age 5). Equipment is included. The school also offers coasteering, kayaking, paddleboarding, hire, camps, and two apartments above the school.
