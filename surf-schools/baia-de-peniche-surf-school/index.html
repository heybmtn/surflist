Baia de Peniche Surf School

Baia de Peniche Surf School is a surf school in Baleal, Peniche, Portugal.

Baleal surf, bodyboard and SUP school and camp founded in 2007 by Peniche local and multi-time European bodyboard champion Silvano Lourenço. Coaching emphasises clear technique explanation, with partner accommodations near the beach and extras such as guiding and Lisbon airport transfers.

Services

  • Surf lessons
  • Bodyboard lessons
  • SUP and wave lessons
  • Surf and bodyboard rentals
  • SUP rentals
  • Surf / bodyboard guiding
  • Accommodation packages (shared, double, private apartments) with lessons or rentals
  • Transfers (including Lisbon airport)
  • Extras: surf shuttle, fishing, land tours, car hire, SUP in Óbidos Lagoon

Lessons

  • Surf and bodyboard group lessons (site aims for ~6 students per teacher; max stated 6–8; weekly group intake capped for quality)
  • Private surf lessons
  • SUP and wave lessons
  • Accommodation + lessons packs: typically 1 x 2-hour surf or bodyboard lesson per day with board and wetsuit, transfers when needed, video feedback on longer packs
  • Optional second daily session (extra surcharge noted for 5-day packs)

Rentals

  • Surfboard
  • Bodyboard
  • Wetsuit
  • SUP
  • Extra accessories (hood, gloves, shoes) on request

Camps

  • Surf or bodyboard camp with shared, double or private apartment stays (3, 7 or 14 nights options described)
  • Accommodation + lessons and accommodation + rental combinations
