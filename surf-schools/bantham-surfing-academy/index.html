Bantham Surfing Academy

Bantham Surfing Academy is a surf school in Bantham, Devon, England.

South Devon's mobile surf school, no longer fixed at Bantham Beach and instead chasing the best conditions around Bigbury Bay and nearby coves. Coaching covers surfing plus paddleboard and kayak sessions with hire available through the same operation.
