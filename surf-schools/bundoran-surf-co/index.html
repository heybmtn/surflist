Bundoran Surf Co

Bundoran Surf Co is a surf school in Bundoran, Donegal, Ireland.

One of Ireland’s longest-running ISA-registered surf schools, open since 2005 from beach bases at Tullan Strand and Rossnowlagh. The Main Street hub also houses a surf lodge and shop, so lessons, rentals and stay packages sit under one roof on the Wild Atlantic Way.

Services

  • Surf lessons (beginner and improver)
  • Kids summer surf camps
  • Surfboard and wetsuit hire
  • SUP tours and Monster SUP
  • Surf lodge and surf & stay packages
  • Dublin bus packages
  • Group surf weekends

Lessons

  • Beginner group lesson (2 hours) — adults and under-16s; Tullan Strand and Rossnowlagh
  • Family-only beginner sessions
  • Improver lessons (max 1:6; min 2 people)
  • Improver weekend (3 coaching sessions over 2 days)
  • SUP tours (approx 2 hours, May–September)
  • Monster SUP (groups of 6–16)

Rentals

  • Softboards
  • Hardboards / performance boards (Channel Islands, Pyzel and others)
  • Wetsuits (5/3mm year-round)
  • Multi-day rental discounts

Camps

  • Kids summer surf camps ages 8–16 (5 days, 11am–1pm) at Tullan Strand and Rossnowlagh
  • Adult 5-day surf camps with lodge stay
