Waverider Surf School

Waverider Surf School is a surf school in Fuerteventura, Canary Islands, Spain.

Small-group Fuerteventura school coached by Nathan, specialising in personalised lessons and guiding rather than large camps. Sessions cover beginners through intermediate plus private coaching and longboard guiding, with optional partner accommodation at Alma Surf Villa in Lajares.
