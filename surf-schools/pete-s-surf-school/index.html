Pete's Surf School

Pete's Surf School is a surf school in Machrihanish, Argyll, Scotland.

Pete’s Surf School has operated on the Kintyre coast since 2014, with ISA Level 1 coaching and Surf Life Saving GB lifeguard cover from Westport Beach. The school focuses on winter-weight kit and small lessons so visitors can learn or hire gear in a wild Atlantic setting near Machrihanish.

  • Surf lessons (group, pairs and 1:1)
  • Surf equipment hire
  • SUP hire
