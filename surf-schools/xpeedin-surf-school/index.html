Xpeedin Surf School

Xpeedin Surf School is a surf school in Somo, Cantabria, Spain.

Beachfront Rip Curl official school and pioneering Somo surf shop with roots since 1977, run by local surfer Nacho García. Lessons run year-round in Spanish, English, French, Portuguese and Italian inside Spain’s first surf reserve.
