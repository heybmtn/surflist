Escuela de Surf Sardinero

Escuela de Surf Sardinero is a surf school in Santander, Cantabria, Spain.

The first surf school in the city of Santander (since 2005), based on El Sardinero beach with a shop and a mobile-school concept that follows the day's best conditions.
