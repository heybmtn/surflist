Rainbow Surf School

Rainbow Surf School is a surf school in Anglet, French Basque Country, France.

Rainbow has taught for decades from a beach cabin on Anglet’s Petite Chambre d’Amour, with views toward the Biarritz lighthouse. Labelled École Française de Surf and staffed by state-qualified instructors, it pairs surf and stand-up paddle sessions with on-beach equipment hire.
