Good People Surf

Good People Surf is a surf school in Zarautz, Basque Country, Spain.

Zarautz school and summer surf camp with a beach-town shop on Nafarroa Kalea and a private campground base at Gran Camping Zarautz. Multilingual coaches teach beginners through advanced in small groups, with equipment hire and group trips available.
