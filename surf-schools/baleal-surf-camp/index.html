    Baleal Surf Camp

    Baleal Surf Camp is a surf school in Baleal, Peniche, Portugal.

    Family-run surf camp and school established in 1993, described as Portugal’s first surf camp, with a beachfront Surf Station on Cantinho da Baía in Baleal. Offers level-separated lessons, kids’ classes, rentals and multi-night packages with hostel or private apartment stays steps from the sand.

    Services

    • Surf school (beginner to intermediate/advanced levels)
    • Children’s surf classes
    • Private surf lessons
    • Surfboard and wetsuit rentals
    • SUP and bodyboard rentals
    • Surf camp accommodation (hostel dorm and private apartments)
    • Surf Station beach facilities (showers, changing rooms, lockers, bar)
    • Transfers to/from Peniche bus terminal (with packages)

    Lessons

    • Weekday group lessons ~1h30; max 5 surfers of the same level per instructor; board, wetsuit and insurance included
    • Weekend group lessons (same ratio)
    • Private lessons daily including weekends (1–4 pax pricing)
    • Beginner Level 1 (first wave / softboards) and Intermediate Level 2 (green waves) courses
    • Children’s classes offered
