Capsurf

Capsurf is a surf school in Capbreton, Landes, France.

Capbreton school run by Philippe and team on Plage Notre-Dame at the Hossegor border, open June to September. One of the town’s longer-standing FFS-labelled schools, with lessons beside the lifeguard post and hire available for a Landes stay.

Services

  • Group and private surf lessons
  • Multi-day stages
  • Bodyboard
  • Equipment hire
  • Groups, CE and colonies on request

Lessons

  • Group lessons (~1h30)
  • Private lessons (1–3 people, ~1h30)
  • Stages (typically 5–6 days, flexible)
  • Intensive stage (10 lessons)

Rentals

  • Foam and hard BIC surfboards (6′ to longboard)
  • Bodyboard
  • Billabong wetsuits
  • SUP via lake partners

Camps
