Latas Surf

Latas Surf is a surf school in Somo, Cantabria, Spain.

Somo and Loredo school and surf camp with 13+ years on the Cantabrian coast, pairing small-group lessons with a highly rated surf-house network. Days often mix surfing with yoga, skate and beachside socials for adults, kids and junior campers.
