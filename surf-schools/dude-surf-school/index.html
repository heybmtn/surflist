Dude Surf School

Dude Surf School is a surf school in Figueira da Foz, Silver Coast, Portugal.

Dude Surf School teaches on Praia do Cabedelo in Figueira da Foz with certified instructors and groups capped at five students per coach. The school covers beginners through intermediates, private sessions, kids clinics, camps with lodging, bodyboard, video analysis and group events.
