Arrifana Surf School & Camp

Arrifana Surf School & Camp is a surf school in Arrifana, Algarve, Portugal.

Arrifana Surf School & Camp has taught on Arrifana Beach for over 18 years, with a clifftop surf house walking distance from the sand and a Billabong-sponsored beach hut open in summer. The operation combines daily coaching, equipment hire right on the beach, and week-long surf-camp stays for mixed international groups.

Services

  • Group and private surf lessons
  • Beach hut surf equipment rentals
  • Surf camp with accommodation
  • WhatsApp / phone bookings

Lessons

  • 2-hour group surf lesson
  • 3-hour group surf lesson
  • Private surf lessons
  • Custom surf experiences tailored by level and schedule

Rentals

  • Surfboard with leash (3 hours or 1 day)
  • Wetsuit (3 hours or 1 day)
  • Bodyboard (3 hours or 1 day)
  • Leash (1 day); wax included with board rentals

Camps

  • Full Surfer Package: 6 nights accommodation + 5 days surf lessons
  • Includes board, wetsuit and sports insurance during lessons
  • Room options: private suite, twin, 3-bed or shared
