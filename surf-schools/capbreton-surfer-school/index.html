Capbreton Surfer School

Capbreton Surfer School is a surf school in Capbreton, Landes, France.

Capbreton school since 2003, French Surf Federation–labelled and Qualité Tourisme certified, teaching kids from six through adults at Capbreton, Hossegor and Seignosse. Team coaches in French, English and Spanish from a base near Santocha Beach on Avenue de Lattre de Tassigny.

Services

  • Kids, teens and adult group lessons
  • Surfari small-group sessions
  • Private lessons and video-assisted coaching
  • Club training
  • FFS surf passports
  • Surf + swimming / Safe Ocean package

Lessons

  • Kids lessons ages 6–9 (max 6, 1.5h, low-tide shore waves)
  • Teen lessons
  • Adult lessons age 16+ (max 8, 1.5h)
  • Surfari (4 or 6 person)
  • Private lessons (up to 4 add-ons) and video coaching (~1h in water)

Camps

  • Multi-day packages (1–10 days) via online shop
