Coast to Coast Surf School

Coast to Coast Surf School is a surf school in Belhaven Bay, East Lothian, Scotland.

Scotland's leading surf school (since 2004) at Belhaven Bay near Dunbar — the closest surf beach to Edinburgh — with lessons, SUP, coasteering and island surf camps.
