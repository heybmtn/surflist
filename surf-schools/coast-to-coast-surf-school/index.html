Coast to Coast Surf School

Coast to Coast Surf School is a surf school in Belhaven Bay, East Lothian, Scotland.

Scotland school founded in Dunbar in 2004, based at Belhaven Surf Centre beside Belhaven Beach and also running island and north-coast camps. The team coaches a local high-performance junior pathway alongside everyday beginner-to-improver sessions and coastal adventures.

Services

  • Surf lessons and courses (beginner through intermediate/longboard)
  • Junior holiday and after-school surf clubs
  • Private surf lessons
  • Bodyboard and surfskate lessons
  • Paddleboarding lessons, tours and SUP XL
  • Coasteering
  • Scottish island and north-coast surf camps
  • Yoga and surf camps
  • Surf and adventure equipment hire
  • Gift vouchers
