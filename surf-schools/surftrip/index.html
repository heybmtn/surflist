Hossegor, Landes, France

Surftrip

Surftrip is a surf school in Hossegor, Landes, France.

Long-running Hossegor school on Plage de la Sud, marking 30 years in 2026 and holding the Qualité Tourisme mark. State-qualified instructors work Hossegor, Seignosse and Capbreton beaches from April to October, with a large hire fleet renewed each season.

Services

  • Group and private surf lessons
  • Kids pitchounes lessons (ages 6–8)
  • Surf stages / multi-day courses
  • Club weekly training (Apr–Oct, excl. Jul–Aug)
  • Equipment hire
  • Groups, CE, EVG/EVJF and colonies

Lessons

  • Group lessons from age 8 (max 8 per instructor, 2h / 1h30 in water)
  • Pitchounes lessons ages 6–8 (max 6, 2h / 1h30 in water)
  • Private lessons (1–3 people)
  • Stage Passion intensive (5 or 6 days, 2×2h/day)

Rentals

  • Surfboards (foam and resin), longboard, bodyboard, fins
  • Full wetsuits and shortys
  • Stand-up paddle via lake partner

Camps

  • Multi-day group courses and Stage Passion packages
