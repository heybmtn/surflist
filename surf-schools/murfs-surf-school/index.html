Murfs Surf School

Murfs Surf School is a surf school in Bundoran, Donegal, Ireland.

Beach-based Bundoran school run by Owen Murphy, a multiple Irish surfing champion who grew up on Donegal’s waves. The Murfs trailer sets up at Tullan Strand with at-the-beach hire, so you skip the walk from town with a board under your arm.

Services

  • Surf lessons (group and private, all levels)
  • At-the-beach board and wetsuit hire
  • Stand-up paddle tours from Mullaghmore
  • Kids summer camps
  • Surf shop / merch
