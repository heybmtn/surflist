Moana Surf School

Moana Surf School is a surf school in Cascais, Lisbon, Portugal.

Moana is a family surf school at Guincho Beach in Cascais, founded in 2003 by coach Alex after years teaching abroad. The beach base pairs lessons with an on-site bar and restaurant, and the team teaches in six languages with pickup options from Lisbon, Cascais, hotels or the airport.

Services

  • Group surf lessons
  • Private surf lessons (1-on-1 and duo)
  • Kids and family surf lessons
  • Kids surf camp (holiday / summer)
  • Surfboard and wetsuit rental
  • Corporate team building
  • Stag and hen surf weekends
  • Door-to-beach pickup
  • Additional outdoor activities (SUP, yoga, kitesurf partners, etc.)

Lessons

  • Group surf lessons (small groups; board and wetsuit included; beginner-friendly)
  • Private 1-on-1 surf lessons (board and wetsuit included)
  • Private duo lessons for two people (board and wetsuit included)
  • Kids and family lessons at Guincho and Carcavelos

Rentals

  • Surfboard and wetsuit rental — 1 hour, 2 hours, half day or full day (hardboards on request)

Camps

  • Kids surf camp in Cascais (May–September / school holidays)
