Lahinch Surf School

Lahinch Surf School is a surf school in Lahinch, Clare, Ireland.

Lahinch's original surf school, run by former Irish champion John McCarthy from the beach hut on the promenade — ISA-affiliated, home of the West of Ireland's first five-day surf camp (since 2002).
