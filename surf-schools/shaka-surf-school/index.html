Shaka Surf School

Shaka Surf School is a surf school in Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain.

Long-running Playa de las Américas school with Canarian Surf Federation-certified coaches and more than two decades on Tenerife’s south volcanic coast. Lessons run in seven languages about 30 m from the beach, with kayak and SUP outings alongside surfing.

Services

  • Surf lessons
  • Kayak excursions
  • SUP lessons
  • SUP excursions

Lessons

  • Semi-private surf (2 or 3 students)
  • Private surf lessons with photos
  • Pack of 3 group surf lessons
  • Pack of 5 group surf lessons
  • Private lessons for children up to 12
  • SUP lessons (2 hours, Bahía de Los Cristianos)
