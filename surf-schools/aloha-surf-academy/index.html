Aloha Surf Academy

Aloha Surf Academy is a surf school in Fuerteventura, Canary Islands, Spain.

Family-run Corralejo academy founded in 2011, focused on structured technique-first coaching with one group per day. Courses blend surf-skate drills, beach and water sessions, and multilingual instruction from head coach Alex.

Services

  • Surf courses (learn and improve)
  • Surf camps
  • Surf guiding / surfaris
  • Private tuition / surfari tips
  • Accommodation packages

Lessons

  • Course «improve your surf» (1–5 days)
  • Surf skate session included in courses
  • Surfaris and private tuition
  • Surf guiding (~90 min in water)
