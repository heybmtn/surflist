Caparica Surf Academy

Caparica Surf Academy is a surf school in Costa da Caparica, Lisbon, Portugal.

Caparica Surf Academy operates from Praia da Rainha (Casa do Sol) in Costa da Caparica. Coaches are described as accredited by the Federação Portuguesa de Surf and by Universidade Lusófona – Surf e Performance (Grau I). The school covers beginners through advanced, kids, SUP and bodyboard, plus summer surf clinics and birthday parties.

  • Private surf lessons — any level; flexible scheduling subject to availability; board and wetsuit provided
  • Kids surf lessons — ages 5 to 8; focus on enjoyment and confidence
