Surf South West

Surf South West is a surf school in Croyde, Devon, England.

Croyde’s original surf school, teaching at Croyde Bay since 1996 from the beach car park. Gold award-winning Surfing England Training Centre of Excellence and adaptive surf centre, with ISA / Surfing England Level 2 coaches, specialist SEN and adaptive instructors, and female-led Girls on Waves sessions under Karma Worthington. Partners with The Wave Project (surf therapy) and Wave Wahines CIC. Facilities: changing rooms, storage, reception, indoor/outdoor seating, toilets. Beach sauna listed as new.
