ANAM CARA Surf School

ANAM CARA Surf School is a surf school in Tapia de Casariego, Asturias, Spain.

Rural tourism complex on the western Asturian coast near Tapia de Casariego, combining hostel and village-house stays with Anam Surf Tech lessons led by Australian coach Nate. Guests can also join larger group sessions and rentals via partner Lampariego Surf School at Playa de Peñarronda.

Services

  • Surf lessons
  • Equipment rental
  • Surf camps
  • Rural accommodation (Anam Cara House hostel and Casa Bágaro)
  • Yoga and mindfulness activities

Lessons

  • Private individual lessons (1h 30 min)
  • Multi-day group courses (1–7 days, 2h/day)
  • Partner group lessons at Lampariego / Playa de Peñarronda
