North Coast Watersports

North Coast Watersports is a surf school in Dunnet Bay, Caithness, Scotland.

North Coast Watersports is run by ISA Level 2 coaches Iona and Finn on Scotland’s North Coast 500, with surf coaching centred on the long sandy bay at Dunnet near Thurso. The same crew previously worked at Blackhouse Watersports on Tiree and Aotearoa Surf in New Zealand, and also lead paddleboard sessions around Dunnet Head when conditions are calm.
