Magic Surf School

Magic Surf School is a surf school in Lacanau, Gironde, France.

Magic Surf School runs Lacanau lessons from a Magic Bus that shuttles small groups a couple of minutes to quieter beaches with better space and fewer crowds. Adrien’s team is known locally for a fun, safety-first style that works for first rides through intermediate coaching, including family and sunset afterwork sessions.

Services

  • Group surf lessons and multi-day courses
  • Kids stages (ages 5–10)
  • Private lessons
  • Magic Family sessions
  • Sunset Afterwork
  • Group / association / corporate bookings
  • Transport to the day’s beach included

Lessons

  • Découverte or reprise — 1×2h
  • Formule 2 jours — 2×2h
  • Stage 5 jours — 5×2h (beginner, intermediate or perfectionnement)
  • Stage Enfant — 5×1h30 (ages 5–10)
  • Private lesson — 1×1h30
  • Magic Family — 1×2h (−10% under 10)
  • Sunset Afterwork — 1×2h30

Prices

Contact Magic Surf School to confirm.

Surf spots served

Quieter Lacanau-area beaches selected daily via Magic Bus

When to go

Main season roughly March–October; closed November–February.
