Westward Waves Surf School

Westward Waves Surf School is a surf school in Westward Ho!, Devon, England.

Operates from a converted horse box on Blue Flag Westward Ho! Beach, with extra kit at Livit Adventures campsite in Abbotsham. Instructors tailor sessions from first-timers to intermediates working unbroken waves out the back.

Services

  • Group surf lessons
  • Private surf lessons
  • Surfboard and bodyboard hire
  • Wetsuit hire
  • Paddleboard hire (enquire)
  • School, residential and university groups

Lessons

  • Group: 2-hour lessons, all kit, about 90 minutes water time
  • Private: 1:1 through larger private groups
  • Times follow Westward Ho! tides
