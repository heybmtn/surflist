Wesurf

Wesurf is a surf school in Bundoran, Donegal, Ireland.

Small Bundoran school founded in autumn 2022 by Calum and Katie, both ISA level-2 coaches with deep outdoor-sports teaching backgrounds. They pick the day’s beach for the group’s level and lean into multi-day courses so guests leave safer and more independent in the water.

Services

  • Group surf classes (beginner, improver, advanced)
  • 5-day surf courses
  • Weekend and bank-holiday courses
  • Private coaching
  • Surf and yoga retreat weekends
