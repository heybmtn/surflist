Blue Ocean Surf School

Blue Ocean Surf School is a surf school in Ericeira, Lisbon, Portugal.

Blue Ocean is a surf school and rental outfit based at Ribeira d'Ilhas inside Ericeira's World Surfing Reserve, run by founder Ulisses Reis with FPS-qualified coaches. Lessons cover beginners through advanced, with board and wetsuit hire available from the beach shop.

Services

  • Group surf lessons
  • Private surf lessons
  • Surfboard and wetsuit rental
  • Surf shop

Lessons

  • Beginner group classes — 2 hours; max 6 per teacher (minimum 3 students); board, wetsuit, insurance and FPS-accredited instructor included
  • Intermediate/advanced classes — 2 hours; max 4 per teacher at the same level (minimum 3 students); prior ocean/level evaluation required; board, wetsuit, insurance and instructor included
  • Private classes — 2 hours; 1–4 people; any level; board, wetsuit, insurance and instructor included

Rentals

  • Surfboard rental (softboards, longboards, high-performance and SUP available)
  • Wetsuit rental
