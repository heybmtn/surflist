Fistral Beach Surf School

Fistral Beach Surf School is a surf school in Newquay, Cornwall, England.

The school says it is the only surf school and hire operator on Fistral Beach, based inside the International Surfing Centre. It has been operating for over 20 years and is open all year.

Group lessons start from age 8, private from age 5. Equipment is included, with on-beach changing, showers and storage. The school describes its hire fleet as the UK's largest dedicated surf hire.

Coasteering listed on the site is run by Bare Feet Coasteering, not in-house.
