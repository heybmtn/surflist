Algarve Watersport

Algarve Watersport is a surf school in Lagos, Algarve, Portugal.

Algarve Watersport has run all-inclusive water-sports camps in Lagos since 2006 from The Kitehouse on Estrada da Albardeira. Guests can focus on surf or switch across kite, wing foil, windsurf, wakeboard and SUP, with meals, yoga, and coaching handled on site.

Services

  • Surf lessons (group, semi-private, private)
  • Kids surf lessons
  • Kitesurf school and camps
  • Wing foil school and camps
  • Windsurf school and camps
  • Wakeboard / Wake Park Lagos
  • SUP tours
  • All-inclusive multi-sport camps
  • Daily yoga
  • Airport transfers (on request)
