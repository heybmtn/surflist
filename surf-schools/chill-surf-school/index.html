Chill Surf School

Chill Surf School is a surf school in Seignosse, Landes, France.

Chill Surf School Family is a laid-back Seignosse school founded by state-qualified coach Loïc, based on Plage du Penon with a surf truck that keeps kit close to the day’s waves. The vibe is friendly and unhurried — families, friends and solo learners share the water while coaches stress ocean reading, currents and safety alongside the fun.
