Murillo's Surf & Bodyboard Academy

Murillo's Surf & Bodyboard Academy is a surf school in São Pedro de Moel, Silver Coast, Portugal.

Murillo's is the longest-running surf and bodyboard school between Figueira da Foz and Nazaré, founded in 2006 in São Pedro de Moel. The academy is certified by Turismo de Portugal and the Portuguese Surf Federation, with a safety-first coaching method that runs from first waves through near-pro levels. Off season the team often relocates lessons to Peniche or Figueira when the home beach is too wild.

Services

  • Surf school packages (beginner N1, transition N1+, intermediate N2)
  • Bodyboard training centre / competition team
  • Gear rentals (surfboards, wetsuits, SUPs, bodyboards)
  • Surf shop
  • Adaptive surfing
  • Surfari and camps (coming soon with accommodation)
