Surf Cascais

Surf Cascais is a surf school in Cascais, Lisbon, Portugal.

Surf Cascais is an FPS-certified surf school and camp based in Murches, Cascais, with Surf Villa and Surf House stays near Guincho and Sintra Natural Park. Lessons run year-round at Carcavelos, Guincho or Praia Grande depending on conditions, with yoga and packaged surf holidays alongside day lessons and coaching.

Services

  • Group surf lessons
  • Private surf lessons
  • Surf coaching / guiding
  • Surfboard and wetsuit rental
  • Surf camp packages (accommodation + lessons + yoga)
  • Private parties / corporate surf events
  • Beach transfers from Cascais centre

Lessons

  • Group surf lessons (2 hours; beginner and intermediate groups by level; all material and insurance included)
  • Private surf lessons (2 hours; 1+ people; material and insurance included)
  • Surf coaching / guiding (2 hours plus transport; Lisbon/Cascais/Ericeira area within ~50 km; photo/film optional)
  • Corporate / private party group sessions (2 hours; tailored)

Rentals

  • Surfboard and wetsuit rental (3 hours, full day or longer; foam 6'0–8'6 and epoxy NSP/TORQ from ~5'11 fish to 9'0 mini-mal)
  • Post-lesson board + wetsuit extra (€25 per person for 2 hours, per FAQ)

Camps

  • Surf Villa in Murches (6 bedrooms: 4 shared & 2 private; kitchen, living room, garden, pool)
  • Surf House in Murches (3 private rooms; ~5 min walk from Villa; shared Villa facilities)
  • Cascais centre apartment (up to 4 guests)

When to go

Open year-round. High season for advance booking roughly June–October; mid/high season March–October usually two daily sessions; low season November–March one or two sessions depending on conditions. Carcavelos favoured in winter for protected beginner waves.
