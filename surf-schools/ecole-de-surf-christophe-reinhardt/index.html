Ecole de Surf Christophe Reinhardt

Ecole de Surf Christophe Reinhardt is a surf school in Guéthary, French Basque Country, France.

Quiksilver-backed family school run by veteran Christophe Reinhardt on the preserved beaches of Guéthary and Bidart, with Erretegia as a favourite local spot. Minibus transfers chase the day's best conditions along the Basque coast.

Services

  • Group surf courses and stages
  • Private and small-group lessons
  • Big-wave outings
  • Stag/hen parties
  • Corporate and group bookings
  • Women-only Mama's Surf Club camps

Lessons

  • Private lessons (max 4 per instructor)
  • Initiation and improver coaching
