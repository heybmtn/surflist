Hossegor, Landes, France

Yo Surf School

Yo Surf School is a surf school in Hossegor, Landes, France.

Year-round Hossegor school that picks quieter spots around Hossegor, Capbreton and Seignosse to match the day’s swell and group level. Coaching covers kids from about 10, teens, adults and seniors, with family sessions, private coaching and a Hossegor surf club offer.

Services

  • Group surf lessons (adults, teens, kids, family, seniors)
  • Private and duo lessons
  • Multi-day surf stages
  • Weekend off-season lessons
  • CE / corporate and EVG/EVJF groups
  • Surfboard hire
  • Hossegor surf club

Lessons
