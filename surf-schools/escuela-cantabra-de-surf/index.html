Escuela Cántabra de Surf

Escuela Cántabra de Surf is a surf school in Somo, Cantabria, Spain.

The first surf school in Spain, on Somo beach since 1991 (Quiksilver & Roxy official) — ISA- and federation-certified, with classes for all ages and a surf house, camp and shop.
