Escuela Cántabra de Surf

Escuela Cántabra de Surf is a surf school in Somo, Cantabria, Spain.

Spain’s first surf school, founded in 1991 by David “Capi” García on Somo beach and still Quiksilver & Roxy official. ISA- and federation-certified coaching covers all ages from five up, with surf camps, skate/SUP options and a beachfront shop.

  • Kids surf courses

  • Bodyboard, SUP and skate lessons
