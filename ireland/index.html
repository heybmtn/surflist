    Surfing in Ireland

    Ireland's Wild Atlantic Way delivers cold-water Atlantic surf down the west and south coasts — from the forgiving beach breaks of Donegal and Sligo to the punchier waves of Clare and Waterford. Consistent swell, dramatic scenery, 5/4 wetsuits year-round, and a famously warm welcome.
