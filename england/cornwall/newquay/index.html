Best beaches in Newquay

Fistral

The town's marquee beach break and the heart of UK surf culture — consistent, punchy and busy.

Watergate Bay

Two miles of open sand just north of town, with room to spread out at most tides.

Crantock

A river-mouth beach across the Gannel, quieter and good for longboarding on its day.

Porth

A sheltered, more forgiving cove that's kinder for beginners when Fistral is maxing out.
