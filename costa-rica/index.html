Surfing in Costa Rica

One of the world's great surf destinations, with warm water and remarkably consistent waves on two coasts: the ultra-reliable Pacific — home to beginner-friendly hubs like Tamarindo and Nosara and heavier breaks at Santa Teresa, Dominical and Pavones — and the Afro-Caribbean south around Puerto Viejo, where the Salsa Brava reef fires in winter.
