Surfing in Vietnam

Vietnam's surf runs on the northeast monsoon (roughly September–March), when the central and south-central coast picks up windswell. Da Nang's My Khe beachbreaks are the country's most consistent, with smaller, seasonal scenes around Mui Ne and Nha Trang's Bai Dai Beach.
