Surfing in New Zealand

Aotearoa's two main islands face two oceans, with cold-water surf year-round — from the black-sand beach breaks north and south of Auckland to Raglan's famous left point, the Canterbury beach breaks around Christchurch, and the far-south breaks of Dunedin's Otago coast.
