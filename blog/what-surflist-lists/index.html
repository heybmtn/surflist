    What Surflist lists — and what it doesn't

    Surflist is a curated directory, not a marketplace and not a reviews site. If we cannot confirm a business from its own official website, it is not listed. That rule is the whole product.

    The confirmation rule

    Every listing is checked against the business's own official website. Social profiles are attached only when we can see they belong to that business. We do not invent a second school to make a town look complete, and we do not guess phone numbers, prices or opening hours.

    If a field is missing on a listing, it is missing because we do not have it — not because the page is unfinished in a way you should read as a secret. Thin and honest beats padded.

    Places exist because listings name them

    There is no separate "add a town" step. A country, region or town appears because a real listing points at it, with the name spelled the same way as every other listing in that place. That is why the URLs look like England / Cornwall / Newquay: they are the place names, not marketing labels.

    A town hub needs at least two listings. One confirmed school is still listed on its own page under surf schools; the town just does not get a hub until there is enough there to be useful.

    Verified is paid. It is not a review.

    The verified badge is a billing state. The site owner turns it on when a business pays. It is not awarded for being thoroughly researched, and it is not a score. Every surf school already has a listing page whether they have paid or not. For shops, stays and services, a verified listing is still what unlocks a full page on Surflist; free listings in those categories show as cards that go out to the business.

    If you treat the badge as "best in town" you will misread the directory. Use the town, the beach, the school's own site, and the details on the page.

    What you will not find here

    • User reviews or star ratings. Other sites do that. We don't.
    • Live surf cams, forecasts or tide charts. Use the tools you already trust.
    • Businesses we cannot confirm. A Facebook page alone is not enough if there is no official site we can check — unless that page clearly is the official presence, which we treat as a last resort.
    • A ranking of schools. Order on category pages is verified first, then place name, then business name — not an editorial top ten.

    Four categories, one trip

    The directory is surf schools, surf shops, places to stay and surf services. That is the trip: learn or hire, buy or repair, sleep near the beach. If you run one of those businesses, get listed. If you want the wider web to mention you — search, publications, AI answers — that is a separate offer on the agency page.

    Who writes this

    Surflist was founded in 2026 by Ben Manton. The about page is the entity home. This blog is for guides that help you use the directory, not for ranking the businesses in it.

    FAQs

    Is every surf school in a town on Surflist?

    No. We list businesses we can confirm. A missing school usually means we have not yet checked it, not that it does not exist. If you run one, ask to be listed.

    Can I pay to be marked as the best school in town?

    No. You can pay for a verified listing, which is a badge and extra prominence. It does not change the confirmation rule and it is not a ranking.
