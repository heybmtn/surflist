What we do

Show up on Google Maps

Most people find surf spots on their phone. We set up and improve your Google Business Profile so you appear in the map results when people search near you. That means good photos, the right categories, opening hours that match your season, and quick replies to questions.

Pages built for every spot and service

We build clear pages for each place you work and each thing you offer — beginner lessons, board hire, surf-and-stay packages, and more. When someone searches “surf lessons in [your town]”, we help that page come up. The more real your pages, the more Google trusts you.

Get listed where travellers look

Tourists trust travel guides, surf directories and local tourism sites. We get your business listed on the ones that matter, with links back to your site. This builds your reputation and helps you rank higher over time.

Turn happy surfers into reviews

Reviews sell surf lessons. We set up a simple way to ask every happy customer for one, at the right moment. We also help you reply to reviews — good and bad — so new customers trust you before they even call.

Turn clicks into bookings

Getting found is only half the job. We check your booking journey on mobile and fix what slows people down. Fewer clicks, clearer prices, an easy “Book now” — so more visitors become paying customers.

Get recommended by AI

More surfers now ask ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini things like “best surf school in [your area] for beginners”. These tools pick their answers from what they find online — your website, your reviews, and the travel sites that mention you. We help make sure your business is the one they name, not your competitor.
