Why this matters

Old search rewarded one thing: a page ranking for a keyword.

So when someone asks where to learn to surf in Cornwall, the answer is shaped by who else mentions you, not just what's on your own site.

If the surf web isn't talking about you, you're unlikely to be in the answer.
